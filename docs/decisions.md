## 2026-11-13 — Change the target behavior to sandbagging

**Decision:** Study whether a contrastive activation direction can cause slight, coherent underperformance on harmless synthetic tasks, and compare it with an explicitly instructed sandbagging condition. Test whether negative coefficients shift performance back toward the honest baseline.

**Reason:** This aligns the project with the user's calibrated-sandbagging research while keeping the activation-steering experiment small and interpretable.

**Interpretation limit:** Underperformance is an outcome; sandbagging implies strategy or intent. A drop in accuracy alone does not establish that intent. Response validity, coherence, and error patterns must be measured.
